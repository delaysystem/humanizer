The Riverside school district is reconsidering its plan to purchase thousands of new laptops for students after bids exceeded the allocated budget. The district had intended to replace its aging fleet of devices with a standardized model featuring improved battery life and durability. However, the lowest qualifying bid came in substantially over budget due to rising component costs and new tariffs. School board members are now weighing several alternatives, including splitting the purchase across multiple vendors, postponing the refresh, or seeking additional funding through a ballot measure.
