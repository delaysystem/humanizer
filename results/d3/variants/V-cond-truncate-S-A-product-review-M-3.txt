The ProMix 550 stand mixer handles demanding doughs with impressive power and stability. Its generous bowl capacity accommodates large batches, and the all-metal drivetrain suggests excellent longevity. In extended testing with weekly bread baking, the motor performed flawlessly. The primary drawback is the cost of attachments, which are sold separately at premium prices. A slight vibration at maximum speed is noticeable though reportedly normal. For serious home bakers, the machine justifies its price through reliable performance. It transforms frequent baking from a chore into a pleasure. Cleanup is refreshingly simple, as the stainless bowl and standard attachments tolerate dishwasher cycles without discoloration.
