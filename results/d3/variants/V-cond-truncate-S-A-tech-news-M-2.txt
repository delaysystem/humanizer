St. Anne's Hospital has completed its recovery from a devastating ransomware attack that disrupted operations for over a week last year. The attack, which entered through a third-party vendor's systems, encrypted critical patient data and forced the diversion of emergency services. Hospital administrators made the difficult decision not to pay the ransom, instead investing millions in rebuilding their entire IT infrastructure. The new systems include advanced network segmentation, mandatory multi-factor authentication, and comprehensive staff training programs. Cybersecurity experts have praised the hospital's transparency in sharing lessons learned from the incident.
