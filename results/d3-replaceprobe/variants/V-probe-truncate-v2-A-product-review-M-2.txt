The PaperGlow 3 is an excellent e-reader that gets the fundamentals right. Its high-resolution display renders text with impressive clarity, and the adjustable warm lighting enables comfortable nighttime reading. Battery endurance is outstanding, lasting many weeks between charges. Water resistance adds welcome durability for poolside or bath reading. The user experience has minor friction points, including prominent store promotions and the removal of physical page-turn buttons. Library integration could be more streamlined. Nevertheless, the core reading experience is superb, and the price is competitive within its category. It earns a strong recommendation for avid readers. The reading software handles highlights, notes, and dictionary lookups smoothly, syncing annotations across devices without fuss.
