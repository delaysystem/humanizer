In late 2022, researchers at a California national laboratory achieved a historic milestone in fusion energy, producing more energy from a fusion reaction than was delivered to the fuel by lasers. This achievement, known as scientific ignition, represented the culmination of decades of research effort. The result requires important context: while the fusion reaction released more energy than the laser light striking the target, the lasers themselves consumed vastly more electricity from the grid. True energy gain measured from wall-plug power remains a distant goal. Nevertheless, the demonstration proved that laboratory ignition is physically achievable, and subsequent experiments have improved upon the initial result.
