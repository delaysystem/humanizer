The ProMix 550 stand mixer handles extremely powerful and consistent dough-like mixtures. Its large bowl capacity allows for mixing large quantities of dough, and the all-metal drivetrain indicates good durability. In an extensive test conducted during weekly bread making, the motor performed flawlessly. The main drawback is the price of the accessories, which are sold at a premium individually. Some vibration is noticeable at maximum speed, but it has been reported as normal. For serious home bakers, this machine justifies its price through reliable performance. It transforms frequent baking from a chore into a pleasure. Cleaning is very easy as the stainless steel bowl and standard accessories withstand dishwasher cycles without discoloration. The pouring shield provided in the box effectively prevents flour clouds at the initial stage.
