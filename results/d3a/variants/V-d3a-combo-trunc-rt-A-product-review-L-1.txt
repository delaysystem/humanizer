Electric cargo bikes promise to replace cars for everyday tasks, and the Halmaster e-cargo makes a strong case. With hundreds of pounds of capacity and long-range dual batteries, it allows for grocery runs, dropping off school children, and easy trips to hardware stores. The step-through design accommodates riders of various heights, making it practical for sharing at home. Practical testing reveals meaningful time savings, as bike routes often avoid traffic congestion that slows down cars. Ownership requires careful consideration. The purchase price is significant, and accessories needed for winter riding add to the cost.
