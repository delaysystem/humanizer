The Riverside School District is re-evaluating its plan to purchase thousands of new laptops for students after bids exceeded the allocated budget. The district had intended to replace its aging fleet with a standard model featuring better battery life and durability. However, even the lowest qualifying bid came in significantly over budget due to rising unit costs and new tariffs. Board members are now considering several alternatives, including splitting the purchase among multiple vendors, delaying the upgrade, or securing additional funds through voter approval. Teachers have expressed strong support for moving forward without delay, citing the poor condition of current devices. The technology department warned that managing multiple models would strain limited resources.
