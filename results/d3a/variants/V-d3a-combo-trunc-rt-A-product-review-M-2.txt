The PaperGlow 3 is an excellent e-reader that handles the basics well. Its high-resolution display displays text with impressive clarity, and the adjustable warm light allows for comfortable reading at night. The battery life is excellent, lasting several weeks between charges. Water resistance provides welcome durability for swimming or reading in the bath. There are minor issues with the user experience, including store promotions and the removal of physical page-turning buttons. Library integration could be smoother. Nevertheless, the core reading experience is excellent, and the price is competitive within its class. This makes it a strong recommendation for avid readers. The reading software handles highlights, notes, and dictionary searches smoothly, syncing notes across devices without any hassle. Font selection and design
