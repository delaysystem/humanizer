The SilenceMax Pro noise-canceling headphones compete in a crowded market. Their active noise cancellation is one of the best available, effectively neutralizing airplane engine noise and other low-frequency sounds. The audio tuning favors popular genres, with strong bass and clear midrange, although classical music listeners may find the treble somewhat subdued. Battery performance meets the manufacturer's extended rating and provides hours of playback from a quick charging minute. Comfort for long-term wear is good for most head shapes, though some users report experiencing pressure during prolonged sessions. The companion app offers useful customization but has occasionally displayed issues with setting retention after updates. Phone call quality is acceptable in quiet environments but struggles against wind.
