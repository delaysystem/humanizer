Electric cargo bikes promise to replace cars for daily tasks, and the Halmaster e-Cargo makes a strong case. With several hundred pounds of rate capacity and long-range dual batteries, it easily handles grocery runs, school drop-offs, and hardware store trips. The step-through design accommodates riders of various heights, making it practical for shared household use. Real-world testing reveals meaningful time savings as bike routes often avoid traffic congestion that slows down cars. Ownership requires careful consideration. The purchase price is significant, and accessories for winter riding add considerable cost.
