The Willow Pour-Over Kettle is a well-designed device for coffee enthusiasts. Its ergonomic handle provides excellent control during pouring, and the integrated thermometer ensures accurate water temperature. The capacity is sufficient for multiple servings. However, the external finish shows wear relatively quickly, and the lid handle gets hot during use. Despite these minor issues, the kettle significantly improves boiling consistency. It's a worthwhile investment for dedicated pour-over brewers. The kettle operates on gas, electric, and induction ranges, adding versatility to various kitchens. Heating is reasonably quick, boiling a full kettle in about five minutes on a standard burner. The spout design deserves praise, delivering an accurate stream that blooms and cascades.
