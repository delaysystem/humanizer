The new data center that Renault plans to build has been approved by the local water authority. The facility will require a significant amount of water for its cooling systems, which has raised concerns among nearby farmers and environmental groups. To address these concerns, operators have committed to water recycling measures and usage limits. Supporters of the project say job creation and tax revenue are key benefits to the region. This data center is part of a growing trend of expanding technological infrastructure in the southwestern United States. Construction is expected to begin early next year, with operations starting within two years.
