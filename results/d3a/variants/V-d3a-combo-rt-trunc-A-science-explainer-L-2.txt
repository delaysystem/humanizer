In late 2022, researchers at the National Laboratory of California achieved a historic milestone in fusion energy. They produced more power from a fusion reaction than they delivered to fuel from lasers. This achievement, known as scientific ignition, represents the culmination of decades of research efforts. The result requires significant background: while the fusion reaction released more energy than the laser light that hit the target, the lasers consumed a lot of electricity from the grid. The actual energy gain, measured by wall-plug power, remains a distant goal. However, the demonstration proved that laboratory ignition is physically achievable, and subsequent experiments have improved the initial result.
