The PaperGlow 3 is an excellent e-reader that handles the basics well. Its high-resolution display displays text with impressive clarity, and the adjustable warm light allows for comfortable reading at night. The battery life is excellent, lasting several weeks on a single charge. Water resistance makes it suitable for reading by the pool or in the bath. There are some minor drawbacks to the user experience, including store promotions and the removal of physical page-turning buttons. Library integration could be more streamlined. Nevertheless, the reading experience is excellent, and its price is competitive within its category. It receives a strong recommendation for avid readers.
