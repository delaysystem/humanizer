The Riverside School District is reconsidering a plan to purchase thousands of new laptops for students after bids exceeded the allocated budget. The district had intended to replace its aging fleet with a standard model featuring better battery life and durability. However, rising unit costs and new tariffs caused even a lower qualifying bid to significantly exceed the budget. Board members are now considering several alternatives, including splitting purchases among multiple vendors, delaying the upgrade, or securing additional funds through a vote. Teachers have expressed strong support for moving forward without delay, citing the poor condition of current devices.
