Maplewood City has officially launched its new fiber internet network, bringing high-speed connectivity to thousands of residents. Completed over three years, the project promises gigabit speeds at a competitive price. City officials say this network will boost economic development and improve the quality of life in the community. Local businesses have expressed enthusiasm for the faster speeds, noting that reliable internet is essential in today's digital economy. The network was funded through a combination of municipal bonds and federal grants. Residents can sign up to receive service starting this month, with installation available within two weeks. Officials believe this project will put Maplewood at the forefront of digital infrastructure.
