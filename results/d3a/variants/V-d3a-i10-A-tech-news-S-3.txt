The local water authority has approved a new data center planned by Renault to build. The cooling systems for this facility require a significant amount of water, which has raised concerns among nearby farmers and environmental groups. To address these concerns, the operators have committed to water recycling measures and usage limits. Supporters of the project say that job creation and tax revenue are key benefits for the region. The data center is part of an expanding technological infrastructure in the southwestern United States. Construction is expected to begin early next year, with operations starting within two years.
