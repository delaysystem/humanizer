The Metropolitan Transit Authority has released a major update to its mobile app, introducing a redesigned interface and new real-time tracking features. The update is the result of two years of development effort aimed at improving the passenger experience. Key improvements include more accurate arrival predictions, service disruption notifications, and integrated trip planning across bus and rail networks. Initial user feedback has been largely positive, with many praising the faster load times and clearer navigation. However, some users have reported issues with account migration and missing features from previous versions. Transit officials have acknowledged these concerns and promised additional updates in the coming months. The app has already been downloaded over 200,000 times since its launch.
