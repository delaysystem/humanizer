The Willow Pour-Over Kettle is a well-designed device for coffee enthusiasts. Its ergonomic handle provides excellent control during pouring, and the integrated thermometer ensures accurate water temperature. The capacity is sufficient for multiple servings. However, the external finish heats up relatively quickly, and the lid handle becomes hot during use. Despite these minor issues, the kettle significantly improves boiling consistency. It's a worthwhile investment for dedicated pour-over brewers. The kettle operates on gas, electric, and induction ranges, adding versatility to various kitchens. Heating is fast enough; it boils a full kettle in about five minutes on a standard burner. The spout design deserves praise, as it delivers an accurate stream, making it easy to control bloom and drawdown phases.
