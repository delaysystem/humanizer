The Metropolitan Transit Authority has released a major update to its mobile application, introducing a redesigned interface and new real-time tracking features. This update is the result of two years of development effort aimed at improving the passenger experience. Key improvements include more accurate arrival predictions, service disruption notifications, and an integrated trip planner across bus and rail networks. Initial user feedback has been largely positive, with many praising the fast loading times and clear navigation. However, some users have reported issues with account migration and missing features from previous versions. Transportation officials have acknowledged these concerns and promised additional updates in the coming months. The app has already been downloaded over 200,000 times since its launch. Privacy advocates have noted improvements in data handling, though they continue to monitor the agency's policies. Officials say that modernization efforts are essential to encourage public transit use. Access improvements include screen-reader compatibility and high-contrast display options, which disability advocates have been requesting for years. Multilingual support now includes twelve languages commonly spoken in the service area. An upcoming update promises fare-cap integration, so frequent riders will never pay more than those with passes.
