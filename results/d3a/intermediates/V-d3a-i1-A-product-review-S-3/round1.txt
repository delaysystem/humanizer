After extensive trial testing, RidgeRunner GTX shoes have proven to be effective but are dependent on the terrain. They perform well on dry, hard surfaces with reliable grip and effective rock protection. Wet weather reveals limitations common to this category; careful footing is required. The waterproof construction keeps feet dry when crossing streams but retains warmth in warmer climates. Potential buyers should note the tight fit and consider sizing up. For winter trail running, they remain a strong option within their price range. It took about fifteen miles of walking before the heel collar became completely soft. Reflective details help with visibility during dark winter runs. The lacing system securely holds the midfoot without pressure points on long descents.
