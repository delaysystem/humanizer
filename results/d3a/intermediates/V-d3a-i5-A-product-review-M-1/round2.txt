The Robovac X9 represents a significant step forward in robotic floor care. Its mapping system quickly learns the layout of a home and supports customized cleaning schedules for individual rooms. The ability to avoid obstacles has significantly improved compared to previous generations, with the unit successfully navigating around common household items. The self-emptying base station considerably extends maintenance intervals. Some limitations remain: the vacuum cannot exceed high limits, and software updates have occasionally reset user preferences. At its premium price, value proposition depends on usage patterns. For homes currently paying for regular cleaning services, the payback period is attractively short. Overall, it truly offers the promise of automated floor management. Pet owners appreciate the brush design that avoids getting stuck, which handled long hair better than previous models in testing. The separately sold mopping attachment works well on sealed hard floors for maintenance cleaning but cannot replace manual mopping to remove stubborn dirt. Noise levels during standard operation allow for normal conversation, although the maximum suction mode is considerably louder. Replacement parts, including filters, brushes, and dust bags, are widely available at moderate prices.
