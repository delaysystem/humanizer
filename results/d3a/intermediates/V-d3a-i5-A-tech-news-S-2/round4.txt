New legislation has been passed to make smartphone repair parts available to independent shops. The law aims to reduce electronic waste and lower repair costs for consumers. Under the new rules, manufacturers must provide screens, batteries, and other components at fair prices. Consumer rights groups have hailed the move as a victory for sustainability and affordability. However, industry representatives have expressed concerns about quality control and intellectual property. Legal experts predict that the law will face court challenges in the coming months. Similar measures are being considered in several other states, indicating a broader national trend towards repairability.
