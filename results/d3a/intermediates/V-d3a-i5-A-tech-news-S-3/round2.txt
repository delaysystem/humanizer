The local water authority has approved a new data center that Renault plans to build. The facility will require significant amounts of water for its cooling systems, which has raised concerns among nearby farmers and environmental groups. To address these concerns, operators have committed to water recycling measures and usage limits. Supporters of the project say job creation and tax revenue are key benefits to the region. The data center is part of a growing trend to expand technical infrastructure in the southwestern United States. Construction is expected to begin early next year, with operations starting within two years.
