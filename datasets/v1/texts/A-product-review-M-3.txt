The ProMix 550 stand mixer handles demanding doughs with impressive power and stability. Its generous bowl capacity accommodates large batches, and the all-metal drivetrain suggests excellent longevity. In extended testing with weekly bread baking, the motor performed flawlessly. The primary drawback is the cost of attachments, which are sold separately at premium prices. A slight vibration at maximum speed is noticeable though reportedly normal. For serious home bakers, the machine justifies its price through reliable performance. It transforms frequent baking from a chore into a pleasure. Cleanup is refreshingly simple, as the stainless bowl and standard attachments tolerate dishwasher cycles without discoloration. The pouring shield, included in the box, effectively contains flour clouds during initial mixing. Speed control offers ten settings with genuinely useful gradations, from a slow stir that folds in delicate ingredients to a vigorous whip for meringues. The tilt-head design locks securely and provides ample access for adding ingredients mid-recipe. Owners report that the machine remains stable on countertops even with heavy loads, thanks to its considerable weight and non-slip feet. Color options span classic neutrals and bolder finishes.
