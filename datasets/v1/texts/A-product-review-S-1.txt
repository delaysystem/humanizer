The SoundArc Buds Lite offer impressive value at their budget price point. Setup is straightforward, with quick Bluetooth pairing and a comfortable fit suitable for exercise. Call quality is adequate for everyday conversations. While bass response is limited and the charging case feels inexpensive, these compromises are reasonable given the cost. Battery life meets the manufacturer's six-hour rating. For users who frequently misplace earbuds, the low replacement cost is a significant advantage. Overall, these earbuds deliver solid performance for casual listening.
