Fifty miles into the RidgeRunner GTX shoes, my verdict is split by terrain. On packed dirt and gravel they are superb — grippy, stable, and the rock plate earns its keep on descents. On wet roots they skate like every other lug pattern I have tried, so adjust expectations, not footwork. The waterproof membrane kept my socks dry through creek crossings but runs warm above sixty degrees. Sizing runs a half-size small; I exchanged my first pair. For shoulder-season trail work, they are the best sub-$150 option I have worn.
