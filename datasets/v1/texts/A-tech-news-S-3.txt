A new data center planned for construction near Reno has received approval from local water authorities. The facility will require significant amounts of water for its cooling systems, raising concerns among nearby farmers and environmental groups. The operator has committed to water recycling measures and usage limits to address these concerns. Supporters of the project point to job creation and tax revenue as major benefits for the region. The data center is part of a growing trend of tech infrastructure expanding into the American Southwest. Construction is expected to begin early next year, with operations starting within two years.
