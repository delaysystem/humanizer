After three years of torn-up sidewalks, the Maplewood fiber project finally switched on its first neighborhood this week. Residents on Elm and Fourth can now order gigabit service from two competing providers, with installation fees waived through March. City engineer Priya Natarajan said the remaining four zones will come online one per quarter, putting the full buildout on track for next fall. The project ran eleven percent over budget after crews hit unmarked utility lines downtown, but the council voted unanimously to cover the gap from the infrastructure reserve. Local cafe owner Tom Becker, whose shop served as an unofficial progress board for frustrated neighbors, said he plans to frame the first speed-test printout.
