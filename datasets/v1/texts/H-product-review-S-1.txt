I bought the SoundArc Buds Lite for $29 expecting very little, and they cleared that bar with room to spare. Pairing took seconds, the fit survived my commute jog, and voices on calls came through clearly enough that nobody asked me to repeat myself. Bass is thin and the case feels hollow, but at this price those are observations, not complaints. Battery life matched the advertised six hours in my testing. If you lose earbuds the way I do — three pairs in two years — these are the ones to lose.
