The Willow pour-over kettle costs twice what my old stovetop kettle did, and my coffee improved just enough to justify it. The counterweighted handle makes slow spiral pours genuinely easy, and the thermometer built into the lid is accurate within a degree of my probe. It holds 900 milliliters, enough for two large mugs. Downsides: the matte coating chips if you look at it wrong, and the lid knob gets hot. Buy it for the pour control, not the looks.
