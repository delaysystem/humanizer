After extensive trail testing, the RidgeRunner GTX shoes prove capable but terrain-dependent. They excel on dry, packed surfaces with reliable traction and effective rock protection. Wet conditions reveal limitations common to the category, requiring cautious footing. The waterproof construction keeps feet dry in stream crossings but retains heat in warm weather. Potential buyers should note the snug fit and consider sizing up. For cold-weather trail running, they remain a strong option in their price range. Break-in took roughly fifteen miles before the heel collar softened fully. Reflective details aid visibility during dark winter runs. The lacing system holds the midfoot securely without pressure points on long descents.
