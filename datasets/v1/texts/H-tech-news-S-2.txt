The state's new repairability rules took effect Monday, and phone shops are already feeling the difference. Manufacturers must now sell replacement screens, batteries, and charging ports to independent stores at the same price offered to authorized chains. At FixWell on Grand Avenue, owner Dana Whitfield said her parts costs dropped nearly a third overnight. Consumer advocates pushed for the law after a 2024 survey found that cracked screens sent over two million otherwise working phones to recyclers. Two manufacturers have filed suit, arguing the pricing clause amounts to a taking, but the attorney general's office says it expects the statute to survive. A hearing is scheduled for December.
