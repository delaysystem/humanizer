The Metropolitan Transit Authority has released a major update to its mobile application, introducing a redesigned interface and new real-time tracking features. The update represents the culmination of a two-year development effort aimed at improving the commuter experience. Key improvements include more accurate arrival predictions, service disruption notifications, and integrated trip planning across bus and rail networks. Early user feedback has been largely positive, with many praising the faster load times and clearer navigation. However, some users have reported issues with account migration and missing features from the previous version. Transit officials have acknowledged these concerns and promised additional updates in the coming months. The app has already been downloaded over 200,000 times since launch. Privacy advocates have noted improvements in data handling, though they continue to monitor the agency's policies. Officials say the modernization effort is essential for encouraging public transit use. Accessibility improvements include screen-reader compatibility and high-contrast display options that disability advocates requested for years. Multilingual support now covers the twelve most commonly spoken languages in the service area. A forthcoming update promises fare-capping integration so frequent riders never overpay relative to pass holders.
