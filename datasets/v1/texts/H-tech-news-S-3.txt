A proposed data center outside Reno cleared its last regulatory hurdle Tuesday when the water authority approved a cooling plan capped at 400,000 gallons a day. The operator, Desert Cloud LLC, agreed to recycle eighty percent of that volume and to curtail operations during declared drought emergencies. Nearby alfalfa growers, who packed three earlier hearings, split on the final deal: some called the drought clause a genuine concession, others said any new industrial draw threatens the aquifer. Construction starts in January and is expected to employ 600 workers at peak. The facility's first server halls are scheduled to come online in late 2027.
