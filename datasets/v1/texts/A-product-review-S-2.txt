The Willow pour-over kettle is a well-designed tool for coffee enthusiasts. Its ergonomic handle provides excellent control during pouring, and the integrated thermometer ensures accurate water temperature. The capacity is sufficient for multiple servings. However, the exterior finish shows wear relatively quickly, and the lid handle becomes hot during use. Despite these minor issues, the kettle meaningfully improves brewing consistency. It represents a worthwhile investment for dedicated pour-over brewers. The kettle works on gas, electric, and induction ranges, adding versatility for different kitchens. Heating is reasonably quick, bringing a full kettle to boiling in about five minutes on a standard burner. The spout design deserves praise, delivering a precise stream that makes bloom and drawdown phases easy to control.
