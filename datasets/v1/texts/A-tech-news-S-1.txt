The city of Maplewood has officially launched its new fiber internet network, bringing high-speed connectivity to thousands of residents. The project, which took three years to complete, promises gigabit speeds at competitive prices. City officials say the network will boost economic development and improve quality of life for the community. Local businesses have expressed enthusiasm about the faster speeds, noting that reliable internet is essential in today's digital economy. The network was funded through a combination of municipal bonds and federal grants. Residents can sign up for service starting this month, with installation available within two weeks. Officials believe this project positions Maplewood as a leader in digital infrastructure.
